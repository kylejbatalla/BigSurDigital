From First Idea to Launch — and Beyond

A simple, proven process designed to get your business online quickly—most websites launch within about a week.

  1. 1

    Discovery & Design

    We learn about your business, your customers, and your goals. Then we create a custom website mockup for you to review. Together, we refine the design until it looks exactly the way you want.

  2. 2

    Building Your Website

    We build your website with clean, fast code—mobile and tablet optimized, tuned for Google Search, and complete with contact forms, maps, and anything else your business needs.

  3. 3

    Testing & Full Demo

    Before launch, we thoroughly test your website across devices and browsers. Together, we'll verify forms, links, speed, and layout to make sure everything works exactly as expected.

  4. 4

    Launch Day

    We handle the technical side of going live: your domain, hosting, security, and getting your website in front of Google, so customers can start finding you online.

  5. 5

    Continuous Iteration & Improvements

    Your website should grow with your business. We keep it secure, updated, and running smoothly while making improvements as your needs evolve. You focus on your business—we'll handle the website.
